Venus is the hottest planet in the solar system.
Even though Mercury is closer to the Sun, Venus is hotter because its thick atmosphere traps heat. Surface temperatures reach about 465°C.

Venus has a very thick, toxic atmosphere.
Its atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide, with clouds of sulfuric acid, making it extremely hostile to life.

A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus.
One full rotation (a day) takes 243 Earth days, while one orbit around the Sun (a year) takes 225 Earth days.

Venus rotates backward compared to most planets.
The Sun would rise in the west and set in the east if you were standing on Venus.
