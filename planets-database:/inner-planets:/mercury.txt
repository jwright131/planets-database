Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun.
It orbits at an average distance of about 58 million km, which is why it has such extreme temperatures.

It has the biggest temperature swings of any planet.
Daytime temperatures can reach ~430°C, while nighttime temperatures drop to ~–180°C because Mercury has almost no atmosphere to trap heat.

A year on Mercury is very short.
One orbit around the Sun takes just 88 Earth days—the shortest year in the solar system.

Mercury is heavily cratered, like Earth’s Moon.
Its surface shows lots of impact craters, meaning it’s been geologically inactive for a very long time.
