Saturn has the most spectacular rings in the solar system.
Its rings are made of ice, rock, and dust—and they stretch out hundreds of thousands of kilometers, but are surprisingly thin.

Saturn could float in water (theoretically).
It’s the least dense planet—if you had a bathtub big enough (casual detail), Saturn would float.

It has a TON of moons.
Saturn has over 140 known moons, including Titan, which is bigger than the planet Mercury.

Winds on Saturn are insanely fast.
Wind speeds can reach 1,800 km/h, much faster than the strongest hurricanes on Earth.
