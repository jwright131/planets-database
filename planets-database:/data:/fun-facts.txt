Mercury has ice—even though it’s close to the Sun.
Ice exists in deep craters near its poles where sunlight never reaches.

Venus spins backward.
The Sun would rise in the west and set in the east if you stood on Venus.

Earth is the only planet not named after a god.
All other planets are named after Roman or Greek gods—Earth isn’t.

Jupiter’s Great Red Spot is a giant storm.
It’s been raging for over 300 years and is big enough to swallow Earth.

Saturn’s rings aren’t solid.
They’re made of billions of chunks of ice and rock, some as small as dust, others as big as houses.

Uranus spins on its side.
It’s tilted about 98 degrees, making its seasons extremely long and strange.

Neptune has the fastest winds in the solar system.
Winds can exceed 2,000 km/h, faster than a jet plane.

Mars has the tallest volcano ever found.
Olympus Mons is nearly three times taller than Mount Everest.
